## Typography (mobile-first scale)
- Headline font stack: `Georgia, 'Times New Roman', serif`
- Body font stack: `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`

| Element | Mobile | Desktop |
|---------|--------|---------|
| H1 / hero | 26px | 32px |
| H2 | 20px | 24px |
| Body | 16px | 17px |
| Small / footer | 13px | 13px |

Line-height: 1.55 body, 1.25 headlines.

## Layout preferences
- Preferred blocks: Header, Hero-Image (or Speaker-Bio for webinars), Body-Multi-Section,
  CTA-Full-Width, Footer. Credibility-forward: room for a short author/expert note.
- Calm pacing; no urgency bars unless a real deadline exists.

## Image direction (feeds gen_image.py)
Clean, bright natural-wellness scenes: fresh herbs and botanicals, simple light wood and linen
surfaces, soft daylight, airy negative space. Calm earthy-green and sand palette with warm
terracotta accents. Credible and editorial, never stock-cheesy. **No text, no logos, and no
people (especially not Dr. Apelian — use approved headshots instead).** Format: jpeg, landscape
1536x1024 for heroes.

## Dark mode
Container darkens to `#222420`, body text to `#e8e8e8`; keep terracotta/gold accents.
