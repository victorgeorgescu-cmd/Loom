# The Forgotten Home Apothecary (FHA) — Context

**Positioning:** Traditional home remedies and herbal preparations, rediscovered. Heritage knowledge
made practical for the modern home.

**Audience:** Health-conscious, preparedness-minded, 45+, values self-reliance and natural living.
Skeptical of over-medicalization; trusts tradition and hands-on knowledge.

## Voice & tone
- Warm, knowledgeable, nostalgic — a trusted elder sharing what worked.
- Plain-spoken, never clinical. Story-led where possible.
- **Do:** "soothe," "support," "ease," "time-tested," "in your own kitchen."
- **Don't:** hype, jargon, fear-mongering, or anything that sounds like a pharmacy ad.

## What emails sell
- The Forgotten Home Apothecary book + companion volumes
- Remedy bundles and seasonal collections
- Subscriber education (recipes, plant profiles) that warms toward an offer

## Compliance (non-negotiable)
- Remedies **manage / support / soothe** — never **cure / treat / heal**.
- No specific medical-outcome promises. No "FDA approved" language.
- Testimonials are illustrative, never typical-result claims.

## CTA phrasing
- Strong: "Discover the Remedy →"
- Soft: "Read the Recipe"
