## Typography (mobile-first scale)
- Headline font stack: `Georgia, 'Times New Roman', serif`
- Body font stack: `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`

| Element | Mobile | Desktop |
|---------|--------|---------|
| H1 / hero | 26px | 32px |
| H2 | 20px | 24px |
| Body | 16px | 17px |
| Small / footer | 13px | 13px |

Line-height: 1.5 body, 1.25 headlines.

## Layout preferences
- Preferred blocks: Header, Hero-Image, Body-Single (story-led intro), Testimonial, CTA-Full-Width, Footer.
- Generous whitespace; one primary CTA. Botanical/editorial feel over hard-sell.

## Image direction (feeds gen_image.py)
Naturalistic vintage-apothecary still life and botanical scenes: dried herbs, amber glass bottles,
aged paper and wood textures, soft natural side-light. Muted earthy palette — deep brown, sage,
honey gold, warm cream. Editorial, nostalgic, slightly faded. **No text, no logos, no modern
packaging, no people.** Format: jpeg, landscape 1536x1024 for heroes.

## Dark mode
Container darkens to `#242424`, body text to `#e8e8e8`; keep honey-gold accents (they read on both).
